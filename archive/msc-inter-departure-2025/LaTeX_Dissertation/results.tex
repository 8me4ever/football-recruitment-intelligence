\subsection{Results Summary}

The experimental results demonstrate significant achievements across multiple evaluation criteria:

\begin{enumerate}
    \item \textbf{Superior Performance}: PSO algorithm achieved 92.8\% accuracy with 96.48\% PR-AUC, representing the highest performance across all algorithms
    \item \textbf{Optimal Parameter Configuration}: Maximum sigmoid steepness ($\alpha = 4.0$) and moderate inflection point ($\tau = 0.61$) with risk multiplier = 0.7
    \item \textbf{Computational Efficiency}: PSO provided optimal performance-efficiency balance (335.3s execution time vs GA's 1444.7s)
    \item \textbf{Comprehensive Metrics}: Achieved 0.8557 Cohen's Kappa and 0.8563 MCC, indicating excellent classification reliability
    \item \textbf{Domain Alignment}: Optimised weights emphasise position-specific critical metrics with appropriate disciplinary risk factors
    \item \textbf{Composite Score Excellence}: 0.9315 composite score demonstrating balanced performance across multiple evaluation dimensions
\end{enumerate}

These results establish the Multi-layer Probability Fusion Algorithm as a significant advancement in sports analytics methodology, providing both theoretical innovation and practical effectiveness for transfer prediction applications.
