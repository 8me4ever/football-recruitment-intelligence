# Recruitment & Squad Planning

This context defines the football terms used to diagnose Beijing Guoan's squad and frame recruitment needs at a dated decision point.

## Squad and roles

**Decision Date**:
The cut-off date for information permitted in a squad-planning snapshot. Data published or observed after this date cannot be used to describe that snapshot.

**Squad Membership**:
A player's evidenced affiliation with Beijing Guoan's first team over a dated interval. Membership is separate from appearing in a match, and uncertain intervals remain explicitly unresolved.

**Nominal Position**:
A player's publicly listed or officially registered broad position. It describes the player's listed identity, not every role played in a match.

**Observed Position Group**:
A broad provider-displayed group for a particular match, such as goalkeeper, defender, midfielder, or forward (or the source's G/D/M/F code). It is match-level evidence and must not be silently substituted for a nominal position or a specific tactical role.

**Observed Role**:
A match-specific deployment supported by public lineup, formation, or appearance evidence. One player may have multiple observed roles; unsupported role assignments remain unknown.

**Appearance**:
A player's participation in a match, distinguished from being named in a squad or substitute list without entering the field.

**No Current-Season Appearance Sample**:
A squad member in the Decision Date snapshot with no observed appearance in the included current-season matches. It does not mean zero performance or prove non-availability.

## Competition and evidence

**Competition-Weighted Performance**:
A separate performance view that gives continental matches greater influence than domestic league matches, while retaining competition-specific results and sensitivity checks. Actual minutes, starts, and appearances remain factual unweighted counts.

**Public Evidence Source**:
A source available to the public, prioritized in this order for membership: official competition/registration material, official club material, then third-party material for cross-checking. Disagreement or unclear date validity is recorded as unresolved.
