About

Chinese Super League, also known as Chinese Super League, is a professional football league in China for men.

There are overall 16 teams that compete for the title every year.

The current holder of the title is Shanghai Port and the team that holds the most titles is Guangzhou FC.

Sofascore tracks live football scores and Chinese Super League table, results, statistics and top scorers.

Competition format of Chinese Super League

The teams are placed in 15 tables, with a promotion and relegation system for the best and worst-performing teams. 1 teams are relegated to the Chinese League 1.

The top 6 team qualifies for the AFC Champions League main tournament or preliminary qualifications.

Additional 6 team qualify for UEFA Champions League Qualification tournament or preliminary qualifications.

Additional 9 team qualify for UEFA Champions League tournament or preliminary qualifications.

Chinese Super League top scorers

The top scorers in the last season of Chinese Super League Chinese Super League 2025 season were Fabio Abreu.Last season top scorer is Fabio Abreu with 28 goals.

Team stadiums and attendance

The team with the largest stadium is Beijing Sinobo Guoan with the capacity of 66161.

The average attendance per game was 24076.

The highest attendance per game in the season Chinese Super League 2025 was 57056.This was during the match between Beijing Sinobo Guoan and Guangzhou Evergrande Taobao.

The total attendance in Chinese Super League 2025 season was 4405999.

TV partners

The largest TV partners for Chinese Super League are China Central Television, PPTV, ESPN+.
